Everything in one place

The little things you do every day,
one click away.

Menew replaces a handful of paid menu bar utilities with one fast, native app built in Swift.

System monitor

CPU with live graph, memory, disk, battery, uptime, temperature and the processes eating your CPU.

Clipboard history

Text, links, images and files. Instant search, filters, pins and a rich preview. Opens with ⌘⇧V.

Quick actions

Keep awake, hidden files, hide desktop icons, eject disks, dark mode, color picker, screenshot to clipboard.

Free up space

Clear caches, logs and developer junk in seconds. A deep scan finds old downloads and large files for you to review.

Internet

Live download and upload, local and public IP, and a real speed test powered by macOS itself.

Notes, snippets & devices

Notes with #tags and Markdown checklists, protected notes that open with Touch ID, and reusable snippets in ⌘⇧V.

Clipboard history

Never lose something you copied.

Press ⌘⇧V anywhere, type a few letters and hit return. It pastes straight into the app you were using.

  • Smart previews — color swatches for hex codes, link previews, images and file icons.
  • Filters & pins — jump between text, links, images and files; keep favorites on top.
  • Password-safe — ignores anything marked as a password by 1Password, Keychain and friends.
⌘⇧V open ⌘1–9 quick paste ⌥↩ plain text
Quick actions

Toggles you'd otherwise Google every time.

Show hidden files, hide the desktop, keep the Mac awake for an hour or pick a color from anywhere on screen — without opening Terminal.

  • Clean keyboard — every key is blocked for 2 minutes, unlock with a click.
  • Clean screen — pure black on every display, double-click to exit.
  • Screenshot to clipboard — it lands right in your history.
Privacy

Your data never leaves your Mac.

Menew is open source, so you don't have to take our word for it.

No telemetry

No analytics, no tracking, no account. Ever.

Local storage

History and notes live in ~/Library/Application Support/Menew.
