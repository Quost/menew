Menew vs CleanShot X: free screenshots with annotations for Mac

Which one fits you

Choose CleanShot X if

  • You share screenshots by link a lot.
  • You need richer annotation and video editing.

Menew is enough if

  • You want capture, annotation and recording without a subscription.
  • You want recordings with sound turned into a transcript and minutes on your Mac.
