Menew vs Maccy and Paste: a free clipboard manager for Mac, and more

Maccy is a lightweight, free and open source clipboard manager, and Paste is a paid one with pinboards and iCloud sync. Menew includes a full clipboard history (⌘⇧V) with snippets, a paste queue and search inside images, as part of a menu bar app that also replaces several other utilities.

Which one fits you

Choose Maccy and Paste if

  • You only want a clipboard manager and nothing else (Maccy).
  • You need your clipboard synced to an iPhone or iPad (Paste).

Menew is enough if

  • You want to find screenshots by the text inside them, read on your Mac.
  • You want snippets, a paste queue and search inside images along with the clipboard.
  • You'd rather install one free app than several.
  • You want it open source and with no account.

Try Menew

Free and open source (GPL-3.0), native for macOS 14 or later, no account and no telemetry. AI features run on your Mac with Apple Intelligence.
