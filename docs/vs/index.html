Menew compared

Menew is one free, open source app for the Mac menu bar that covers the everyday part of several utilities. Here is how it compares, including where the specialized apps go further.

Menew vs Raycast

How Menew compares with Raycast: command palette, clipboard history, window management, snippets and AI. Free, open source and on-device, without an extension store.

Menew vs Maccy and Paste

How Menew's clipboard history compares with Maccy and Paste: search, pins, snippets, paste queue and privacy. Free and open source.

Menew vs Rectangle and Magnet

How Menew's window manager compares with Rectangle and Magnet: halves, thirds, corners, drag to edges and the same keyboard shortcuts.

Menew vs iStat Menus and Stats

How Menew's system monitor compares with iStat Menus and Stats: CPU, GPU, memory, disk, network, battery, temperatures, history and alerts.

Menew vs CleanShot X

How Menew's screenshots compare with CleanShot X: annotations, blur, pinning, screen recording with sound, GIF, scrolling capture and copying text from the screen.

Menew vs Otter and Granola

How Menew's recordings compare with Otter and Granola: on-device transcription, meeting minutes, tasks and privacy, with no bot and no account.
