Menew vs Raycast: a free, open source Raycast alternative for Mac

Raycast is a polished launcher with a huge extension store. Menew covers the everyday part of it (command palette, clipboard history, snippets, window snapping, script commands) and adds a system monitor, screenshots and meeting minutes, all free, open source and with AI that runs on your Mac.

Which one fits you

Choose Raycast if

  • You rely on extensions for Jira, GitHub, Notion, Spotify and other services.
  • You want settings and snippets synced across several Macs.
  • You prefer chatting with cloud AI models.

Menew is enough if

  • You want the everyday launcher features plus clipboard, windows, system monitor and screenshots in one free app.
  • You want AI that never leaves your Mac and no account.
  • You want an open source app you can read and change.

Try Menew

Free and open source (GPL-3.0), native for macOS 14 or later, no account and no telemetry. AI features run on your Mac with Apple Intelligence.
