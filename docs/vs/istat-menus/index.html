Menew vs iStat Menus and Stats: a free system monitor for the Mac menu bar

iStat Menus is a paid, very detailed system monitor and Stats is a free, open source one. Menew shows CPU, GPU, memory, disk, network and battery in the menu bar, with temperatures, history, alerts and a plain-language diagnosis, inside an app that also replaces several other utilities.

Which one fits you

Choose iStat Menus and Stats if

  • You want every sensor and very detailed per-core readings.
  • You want the deepest menu bar customization for monitoring.

Menew is enough if

  • You want a clear picture of your Mac, with history, alerts and a plain-language diagnosis, without installing a separate monitor.
  • You want to see where your battery wears out.
