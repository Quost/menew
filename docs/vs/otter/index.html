Menew vs Otter and Granola: private meeting minutes on your Mac

Otter and Granola are cloud services for meeting notes, with team sharing and integrations. Menew records your call (Mac audio and microphone), transcribes it on your Mac and writes minutes with the main topic, each topic discussed, decisions, tasks and next steps, with no bot joining the call, no account and nothing uploaded.

Which one fits you

Choose Otter and Granola if

  • Your team shares and edits notes together.
  • You need every speaker identified by name, or notes on your phone.

Menew is enough if

  • Your meetings are confidential and you don't want audio in a cloud.
  • You want minutes and tasks for free, without a bot in the call.
