Command palette

One box for apps and quick answers.

Apps, Menew actions, snippets and calculations in one place. It never touches the network until you ask for a currency rate.

CtrlAltSpace open Ctrl1–9 run the Nth result Tab apps or clipboard

Apps and recent picks

Finds your installed apps and puts what you picked recently first.

Math, units and currencies

12*7.5, 20% of 340, 5 km to mi, 72 f to c. Currencies (100 usd to brl) use the European Central Bank rate, fetched only when you press Enter.

Time zones

3pm ny to lisbon, time in Tokyo or just a city. A pasted Unix timestamp shows its date.

Text transforms

20 transforms on the copied text (case, accents, sort, JSON, Base64, URL, counts), with a live preview.

Quick links

g coffee, gh menew, maps paulista, yt, wiki and your own. A typed address opens in your browser.

Emoji and symbols

emoji rocket, :fire, arrows, degrees and more, found by name and ready to paste.

Timers, alarms and focus

timer 10m tea, alarm 7:30 meeting, focus 50. The countdown shows in the tray, with a notification and a sound at the end.

Script commands

Every script in a folder (shell, Python, Node, Ruby) becomes a command, and Raycast script headers work as they are. Scripts only run when you pick them.

Clipboard history

Never lose what you copied again.

Text, links, images and files with instant search, filters, pins and a preview. Passwords from password managers are never saved.

CtrlAltV open CtrlAltN paste next

Search and pins

Instant search, pinned entries first, then the most recent, each with a one-line preview and its age.

Images and files

Kept too, with a thumbnail and a preview pane. Filters for All, Text, Links, Images, Files, Pinned and Snippets.

Text inside images

Copied images are read on your computer with Tesseract, so search finds the words in them. Nothing is uploaded.

Smart actions

Call a number, write an email, open an address, track a package, copy a color as HEX, RGB or HSL, or join a meeting.

Pause and passwords

Pause the capture for 15 minutes, 1 hour or until you resume: nothing is read while paused. Copies flagged by password managers are skipped.

Snippets

Texts with {date}, {clipboard}, {cursor} and {input:Name}, in the palette and in the Snippets filter.

Paste next

Turn on the paste queue, copy several things, then run Paste next once per item, in the order you copied them.

Paste for you, if you allow

By default Menew copies the entry and tells you to press Ctrl+V. With write access to /dev/uinput (off by default) it presses the keys for you.

Linux requirements

What you need.

Menew is built for Wayland. So far it was tested only on headless GNOME Shell 46 and KWin 5.27 sessions (Ubuntu 24.04), not on a real desktop yet.

Setup notes

  • Desktop: GNOME or KDE Plasma on Wayland. X11 is best effort only.
  • Shortcuts: the GlobalShortcuts portal (KDE Plasma and GNOME 48+). On GNOME 45 to 47 you create two custom shortcuts.
  • Clipboard on GNOME: a small Menew GNOME Shell extension sends the copies to Menew. You enable it once and log out and in. KDE needs nothing extra.
  • Tray icon on GNOME: install the AppIndicator extension. KDE Plasma shows it out of the box.
  • Auto paste: optional and off by default. It needs write access to /dev/uinput.
Privacy

The same promise as on the Mac.

No telemetry, no analytics, no account. The clipboard history stays on your computer.

0telemetry
0accounts
100%on your computer

Stored locally

The history lives in a local SQLite file and images are files in your data folder. Nothing is uploaded.

Passwords are skipped

Copies flagged by password managers are never read, and nothing is read while the capture is paused.

Images are read here

The text inside images is read with Tesseract on your computer.

The only network request

  • A currency rate, only when you press Enter on a conversion
  • One HTTPS request to api.frankfurter.app with the two currency codes and nothing else
  • Typing never sends anything
  • Quick links open in your browser; Menew does not contact those sites
