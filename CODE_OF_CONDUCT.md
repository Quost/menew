# Code of conduct

Menew follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

## Our pledge

We want Menew to be a welcoming place for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and take feedback gracefully; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Help newcomers. Everyone was new once.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Any other behavior that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent the project.

To report a problem, use [private reporting](https://github.com/Quost/menew/security/advisories/new) in the Security tab and mention it's about conduct. Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may remove comments, close threads, or block people who break these rules, following the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
